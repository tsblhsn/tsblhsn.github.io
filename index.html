A Humble Beginning

Hello, I am Tasin Ahmed. I am currently a 12th-grade Humanities student at Milestone College. My journey has been shaped by a blend of creative and solitary pursuits. I spent about two years working as an assistant writer in the Bangladeshi media and drama industry. After a brief pause, I am deeply grateful to have resumed this work in 2026.

I find profound peace in solitude—wandering on my own, immersing myself in music, and capturing fleeting moments through photography. I also hold a genuine interest in exploring politics and philosophical thoughts. Thank you for visiting my small corner of the internet; I am constantly learning and thankful for the journey ahead.
