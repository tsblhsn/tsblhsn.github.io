About Tasin

Born on February 12, 2007, currently residing in Dhaka, Bangladesh, Tasin Ahmed is a dynamic individual balancing his academic life as a student at Milestone College and his entrepreneurial journey as the founder of Claye BD. Driven by a profound sense of leadership and purpose, Tasin harbors a deeply rooted dream of becoming the Prime Minister of Bangladesh. Alongside his grand vision for the nation, he is passionately invested in exploring the concepts of time travel, creating films, and developing original philosophical ideas.

Claye BD

  • Food: Biscuits, drinks
  • Essential Goods: Salt, sugar, cement, and more
