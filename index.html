Biography

I am Tasin Ahmed (@tsblhsn), currently studying in the 12th grade Humanities department at Milestone College.

I spent around two years working as an Assistant Scriptwriter in Bangladeshi television dramas and media production. After taking a brief break, I restarted my writing journey in 2026.

"It's hard." I prefer keeping things grounded without complex pretense. Outside of work and study, I enjoy wandering alone, listening to music, and exploring politics, philosophy, and photography.
